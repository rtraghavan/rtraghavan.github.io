<span class="newthought">This is a look back</span> at the work that occupied me before 2025. Broadly, I have been interested in the principles governing the function of the primate visual and visuomotor system. Below are the projects I worked on — much of it in collaboration with other experimentalists and theorists, whom I name where relevant.
